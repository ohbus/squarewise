## Objective

Bound GraphQL resource consumption and subscription accumulation so authenticated
clients cannot exhaust BFF CPU, memory, or reactive queues with deeply nested,
high-complexity, or unbounded subscription workloads.

## Acceptance criteria

- GraphQL depth and calculated complexity limits are configurable and enforced
  before resolver execution.
- Per-user subscription counts are bounded and expired subscriptions do not
  consume capacity.
- Queue capacity and subscription TTL remain bounded and configurable.
- Focused tests prove query instrumentation wiring and subscription rejection.
