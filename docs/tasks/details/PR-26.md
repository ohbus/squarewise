## Objective

Reject financial mutations that reference non-members or exceed bounded input
limits before ledger side effects are written.

## Acceptance criteria

- Every payer and allocation participant is an active group member or active
  placeholder in the locked mutation transaction.
- Duplicate participant IDs and invalid payer/allocation totals are rejected.
- Shared request limits bound participant counts, text lengths, amounts, and
  idempotency keys at the validation boundary.
- Focused rejection tests prove no postings are committed for invalid input.
